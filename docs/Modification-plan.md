### HEALTH INSIGHTS

A comprehensive health report generated by an advanced AI model, based on a thorough review of the member’s past medical history, including chronic conditions, family medical history, doctor visits, prescriptions, and lab results. 



DOCTOR VISIT REPORT  
  
A doctor visit report should be prepared by summarizing the key discussions, diagnoses, and recommendations from the consultation. It must include:  
\- \*\*Patient Information\*\*: Name, age, and medical history.  
\- \*\*Reason for Visit\*\*: Symptoms or concerns addressed.  
\- \*\*Diagnosis\*\*: Conditions identified or ruled out.  
\- \*\*Treatment Plan\*\*: Prescriptions, tests, or follow-up actions.  
\- \*\*Doctor's Notes\*\*: Observations and advice provided during the visit.  
\- \*\*Follow-Up\*\*: Next steps, referrals, or scheduled appointments.

### PRE-CONSULTATION NOTE

A pre-consultation note should be prepared by systematically capturing the following details:

- **Chief Complaints**: Current symptoms, discomforts, or issues the patient is experiencing.
- **History of Present Illness**: Duration, severity, and progression of complaints.
- **Past Medical History**: Previous diagnoses, surgeries, or chronic conditions.
- **Medication History**: Current and past medications, including dosages and adherence.
- **Allergies**: Known allergies to medications, foods, or environmental factors.
- **Family Medical History**: Relevant hereditary conditions or risks.
- **Lifestyle Factors**: Diet, exercise, smoking, alcohol, or stress levels.
- **Clarifications Required**: Specific questions or concerns the patient wants addressed during the consultation.
